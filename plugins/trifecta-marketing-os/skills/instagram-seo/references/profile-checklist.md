# Instagram profile SEO checklist

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account type | Professional (business or creator) |
| 2 | Visibility | Public |
| 3 | Search engine indexing | Allowed in settings (Settings and privacy > Account privacy / Sharing and reuse; label varies by app version, so confirm on the device) |
| 4 | Name field | Brand name + primary keyword, e.g. "Brand | Primary keyword"; within 64 chars |
| 5 | Username | Simple and searchable; keep as is if established (changing a handle costs recognition) |
| 6 | Bio | Primary keyword in the first line, who it's for, proof point, CTA; within 150 chars |
| 7 | Link | Tracked link (UTM) to the most relevant landing page, not just the homepage |
| 8 | Category | Matches the business (e.g. Manufacturer, Product/Service) |
| 9 | Contact / location | City listed where local search matters |
| 10 | Highlights | Named by buyer need or persona, keyword-friendly titles, cover style on brand |
| 11 | Pinned posts | 3 pinned: best proof, best offer/CTA, best story |
| 12 | Alt text backfill | Top 12 to 20 posts by reach have custom alt text |
| 13 | Recent captions | Average Mode B score 80+ over last 20 posts |

Settings marked by the user only: the plugin cannot change account settings. List the steps and ask the user to confirm once done.
