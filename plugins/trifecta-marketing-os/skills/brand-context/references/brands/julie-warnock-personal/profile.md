## Mission / Positioning
"Taste plus operations. Creative vision meets production reality in modern LA manufacturing." The mission is to establish Julie Warnock as a recognized female voice in modern American manufacturing, creative branding, and founder-led business building — while connecting Bath Promotions and Custom Candles LA into a coherent, trust-building ecosystem. This is the concrete source-material confirmation of the founder-loop relationship in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §4.2 — it's explicitly designed into the brand, not something this AI OS invented.

**It is:** a founder voice bridging luxury taste with manufacturing systems; a practical storyteller for creative operators and brand builders; a leader translating sensory branding into production, distribution, and growth.
**It is explicitly not:** influencer content, lifestyle fluff, humble-bragging, generic entrepreneurship content, or founder-influencer noise.

## Ideal Client / Persona (Audience)
- **Primary:** founders, creative entrepreneurs, brand builders, distributors, marketers, industry peers across promotional products, hospitality, and luxury goods.
- **Secondary:** press, podcast hosts, conference organizers, prospective partners, talent, future investors.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** founder-operational — Cinematic, Intelligent, Aspirational, Grounded, Real, Direct, Visionary, Occasionally Funny, Candid, Observational, Systematic.

**Do:** share manufacturing lessons with clarity; link taste decisions to operational tradeoffs; lead with real problems and practical outcomes.
**Don't:** generic founder-influencer language; buzzword-heavy startup speak; aspirational fluff without operational truth.

**Banned terms:** influencer, lifestyle, fluff, hack, guru, generic entrepreneur.

**Signature phrases:** "Woman building an empire in an LA warehouse." · "Taste + operations. Both. Always." · "The details are everything." · "I'm building a modern manufacturing company with a cinematic soul." · "Luxury is not just how it looks. It's how it's made and how it makes you feel."

**CTA style:** invitation to learn/join/build with systems — e.g. "Read the founder playbook," "See the systems behind the brand," "Subscribe for manufacturing and sensory strategy."

## Visual Direction
Documentary, cinematic, founder-in-motion imagery grounded in LA manufacturing. Natural, editorial, warm industrial light with honest production texture. Palette: deep neutrals, charcoal, warm wood, amber highlights. Textures: concrete, glass, brass, leather, paper, machinery, tool marks. **Avoid:** influencer lifestyle fluff, glossy aspirational filters, generic entrepreneur branding.

**Brand colors:** Deep Slate Blue `#3B5A75` (primary), Muted Periwinkle `#A6B9CB` (secondary), Ice Blue `#C7D6E4` (light accent), Chalk White (background).
**Typography:** Sofia Pro (full weight range, UltraLight–Black, with italics).
