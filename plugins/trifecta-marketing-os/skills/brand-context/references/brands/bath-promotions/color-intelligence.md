# Bath Promotions — Color Intelligence (GAP)

**This is a documented gap, not a finished asset.** Unlike Custom Candles LA ([[custom-candles-la color-intelligence]], 46 colors) and Julie Warnock ([[julie-warnock color-intelligence]], 43 colors), Bath Promotions has **no dedicated `color_intelligence.yaml`** in the Marketing OS. Confirmed by direct search of `02-marketing-os/MARKETING OS/services/brand_intel/archives/bath_promotions/` — no such file exists.

## What *does* exist for BP
The shared [[color-intelligence-methodology]] includes a Bath Promotions application guide (Section 4), which is the only BP-specific color guidance currently in the system:
- **Primary layers:** material origin, cultural/political charge (not the psychological/sensory layers CCLA uses).
- **Priority colors:** khaki, umber, sienna, lead white, cobalt, silver, Prussian blue, amber, gold.
- **Register:** B2B / distributor-facing. Color intelligence here serves **product authority and trade-education content** ("here's what it took to make this color, and why that means quality"), *not* emotional lifestyle positioning — consistent with the brand's trade-savvy-expert voice ([[bath-promotions profile]]).

## Why the gap makes sense (and when to fill it)
It's not an oversight so much as a reflection of use: CCLA and Julie generate color-driven *content* constantly; BP sells manufacturing capability to distributors, where color is a product spec more than a story. Whether to build out a full BP color-intelligence file is a **business decision for Julie**, not a migration task — worth doing only if BP's content strategy starts leaning on color-as-authority storytelling (the methodology's "material origin → trade education" angle). Flagged here so the gap is visible and deliberate, per `_system/documentation-standards.md`.
