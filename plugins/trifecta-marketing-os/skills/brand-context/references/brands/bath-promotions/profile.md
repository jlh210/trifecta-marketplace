## Mission / Positioning
"Industrial precision meets strategic sales intelligence. We make things that make your clients look extraordinary." Bath Promotions is a distributor-facing manufacturing partner for premium branded sensory products (bath, body, home fragrance) — and a sales-enablement partner that helps distributors pitch and sell with technical confidence, not just a supplier.

**It is:** a capable, LA-based production expert; a sales-enablement partner for distributors.
**It is explicitly not:** a spa brand, a luxury lifestyle brand, a mass-market gift supplier, a passive catalog vendor, an Etsy seller, a wedding vendor, cheap swag, or spa-like/overly feminine/influencer-coded.

## Ideal Client / Persona
- **Primary:** promotional products distributors who need reliable branded product solutions.
- **Secondary:** corporate buyers, advertising agencies, and businesses sourcing premium promo products through distributor networks.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** trade-savvy-expert — Expert, Capable, Insider, Confident, Tactile, Efficient, Elevated, Smart, Clear, Direct.

**Do:** show manufacturing process as proof of capability; explain why customization/decoration choices matter; use distributor-facing sales language.
**Don't:** fluffy spa/wellness language; cheesy promo/gimmicky copy; vague corporate jargon; the word "craft"; "vertical" (use "industry"); "line card" (use "pitch deck").

**Banned terms:** craft, crafting memories, spa, wellness, gimmicky, cheap, mass-market.

**Signature phrases:** "Premium sensory products made sellable." · "Made to order in L.A." · "Premium merch. Made to stand out." · "How it's made." · "How to sell it." · "Not just promo, a branded experience."

**CTA style:** direct, capability-first, distributor-ready — e.g. "Save this for your next hospitality pitch," "Request a decoration recommendation."

## Visual Direction
Product documentation elevated — process shots, machinery, hands, production tables. Industrial natural light, bright even tone, clean warehouse compositions. Palette: steel blues, warm grays, natural whites, kraft paper tones. Textures: glass, metal, paper, kraft, concrete, wood. Clean, minimal grading, honest representation. **Avoid:** over-filtered spa aesthetics, feminine styling, generic stock photography, pastel filters, Etsy vibes.

**Brand colors:** Deep Navy Blue `#196D9A` (primary), Bright Sky Blue `#27AAE1` (secondary), Pale Ice Blue `#E3F2F7` (light accent), White/Off-White (backgrounds).
**Typography:** Bricolage Grotesque (headlines/display, condensed/semicondensed), Figtree (body).
